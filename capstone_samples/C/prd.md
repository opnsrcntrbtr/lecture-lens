# TutorTrail: PRD

## Problem
Students get stuck on homework and parents cannot always help. The market for homework help is huge and growing fast.

## Users
Secondary-school students studying mathematics at home.

## Requirements and acceptance criteria
- Hints should be high quality and engaging.
- The bot should understand what the student means.

## Evaluation
The model should perform well on our tests and be highly accurate.

## Trust architecture
TutorTrail gives hints, never final answers. If the student asks for the answer twice, or the model's confidence in its reading of the problem is low, it shows the worked method from the textbook chapter and suggests asking the teacher. A wrong hint can teach a wrong method before an exam, so every hint cites the textbook section it follows.

## Launch plan
Launch to all students at the start of term.
